Engagement options

Can Vertic Security support advisory and installation?

Yes. The engagement can cover advisory, design and delivery, or a defined portion of the lifecycle. Exact delivery responsibility depends on project scope, location, licensing, platforms and approved partners.

Can Vertic Security review an existing design or installation?

Yes. Independent reviews can assess requirements, coordination, constructability, installation quality, testing evidence and operational readiness.

Can Vertic Security support upgrades in operational facilities?

Yes. Modernisation planning can consider staging, continuity, migration, interfaces, commissioning and handover requirements.
