Core services

Capability across the security lifecycle.

Engage a focused workstream or combine services into an integrated advisory, design and assurance package.

01

Security advisory

Strategies, risk assessments, governance frameworks and project briefs that establish priorities, responsibilities and measurable outcomes.

  • Security strategies and master plans
  • Threat, vulnerability and risk assessments
  • Operational and portfolio reviews
02

Physical security design

Coordinated designs for access control, video surveillance, intrusion detection, intercom, perimeter protection and supporting infrastructure.

  • Concept through detailed design
  • Drawings, specifications and device schedules
  • Multidiscipline design coordination
03

Delivery support

Technical support from procurement through installation, testing and handover, helping project teams maintain design intent.

  • Tender and scope reviews
  • Submittal and shop drawing assessment
  • Commissioning and acceptance support
04

Security assurance

Independent review and verification activities that identify gaps, confirm evidence and support informed acceptance decisions.

  • Design and constructability reviews
  • Installation quality surveillance
  • Defect and evidence tracking
05

Modernisation planning

Structured assessments and migration roadmaps for ageing, fragmented or capacity-constrained security environments.

  • Current-state assessments
  • Technology and lifecycle roadmaps
  • Staging and transition planning
06

Standards and governance

Practical standards, templates and assurance controls that improve consistency across projects, sites and delivery partners.

  • Technical standards and guidelines
  • Design and commissioning templates
  • Compliance and decision registers
