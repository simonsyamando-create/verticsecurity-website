Complete security deployment

End-to-end electronic and physical security systems.

Vertic Security supports the complete deployment lifecycle, from risk and design through installation, integration, commissioning and ongoing system improvement.

01

Access control systems

Door controllers, readers, credentials, locks, request-to-exit devices, emergency releases, lift control and access permissions.

  • Door hardware coordination
  • Access levels and schedules
  • Alarm and event programming
02

CCTV and video surveillance

Camera selection, coverage design, recording, monitoring, analytics, retention and operator viewing requirements.

  • Fixed, multisensor and PTZ cameras
  • Video management and recording
  • Coverage verification and testing
03

Alarm and intrusion systems

Detection and alarm solutions for buildings, secure rooms, perimeters and critical assets.

  • Door contacts and motion detection
  • Duress and panic alarms
  • Alarm routing and escalation
04

Intercom and communications

Integrated communication for entrances, gates, lifts, loading areas, help points and high-security zones.

  • Audio and video intercom
  • Call routing and recording
  • Access-control integration
05

Perimeter security

Layered protection combining physical barriers, electronic detection, surveillance, lighting and response workflows.

  • Fence and gate monitoring
  • Perimeter intrusion detection
  • Vehicle and pedestrian control
06

Security networks and infrastructure

Cabinets, power, network architecture, fibre and copper pathways, resilience and system segregation.

  • Security network design
  • UPS and power coordination
  • Rack and field infrastructure
