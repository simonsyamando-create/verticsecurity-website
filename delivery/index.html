Delivery lifecycle

Design intent carried through to operational handover.

Delivery support provides structured control of interfaces, installation quality, configuration, commissioning evidence and acceptance.

01

Mobilisation and preconstruction

Validate scope, responsibilities, programme, submissions, interfaces, procurement dependencies and acceptance requirements.

02

Technical coordination

Resolve doors, power, network, pathways, architecture, fire, lifts, control rooms and operational interfaces.

03

Installation assurance

Review workmanship, mounting, labelling, cabling, configuration, change control and evidence against approved requirements.

04

Integration and programming

Coordinate alarm behaviours, access permissions, video workflows, intercom routing and system-to-system interfaces.

05

Testing and commissioning

Verify devices, scenarios, failover, response workflows, performance, defects and acceptance evidence.

06

Handover and transition

Complete as-built records, schedules, training, support information, outstanding actions and operational readiness.
