Capability statement

Protecting critical infrastructure through better security design.

Company overview

Vertic Security is an Australian security consultancy providing independent strategy, risk advisory, physical security design, technology advisory and assurance services. We help clients define risk, make informed decisions and deliver practical security outcomes aligned with operational objectives.

Core capability

Security consulting

Strategy, master planning, governance, standards and project advisory.

Risk advisory

Threat, vulnerability, risk and gap assessments with prioritised treatments.

Physical security design

Access control, video surveillance, intrusion detection, intercom, SOC and infrastructure.

Security assurance

Reviews, audits, construction support, testing and commissioning assurance.

Government and SCEC advisory

Protective security, secure-facility, security-zone and applicable pathway support.

Technology advisory

Vendor-neutral strategy, integration, migration and lifecycle planning.

Industry focus

Data centres • Critical infrastructure • Government • Commercial property • Healthcare • Education • Financial services • Industrial facilities
