Security consulting.

Company overview

Vertic Security is an Australian security consultancy providing independent security consulting, risk advisory, physical security design, technology advisory and assurance services. We help clients define risk, make informed investment decisions and deliver practical security outcomes aligned with operational and business objectives.

Core services

Security consulting

Strategy, master planning, governance, standards and project advisory.

Risk advisory

Threat, vulnerability, risk and gap assessments with prioritised treatment plans.

Physical security design

Access control, video surveillance, intrusion detection, intercom, SOC and supporting infrastructure.

Security assurance

Independent reviews, audits, construction support, testing and commissioning assurance.

Government and SCEC advisory

Protective security, secure facility, security-zone and applicable certification pathway support.

Technology advisory

Vendor-neutral technology strategy, integration, migration and lifecycle planning.

Sectors

Data centres • Critical infrastructure • Government • Commercial property • Healthcare • Education • Financial services • Industrial facilities

Why Vertic Security

  • Independent and vendor-neutral advice
  • Risk-led, operationally grounded recommendations
  • Clear, coordinated and traceable deliverables
  • Experience supporting complex and high-consequence environments
  • Support from early strategy through delivery and assurance
