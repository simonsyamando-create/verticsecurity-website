How Vertic Security collects, uses and protects personal information.

Privacy Policy

Vertic Security respects the privacy of people who contact us. This policy explains how we handle personal information received through this website, email, telephone and project enquiries.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, organisation, email address, phone number, project location, requested services and information included in your enquiry or project brief.

How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, assess proposed engagements, prepare proposals, communicate about services and meet legal, regulatory and security obligations.

Disclosure and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, communications and enquiry workflow. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Access, correction and complaints

To request access to or correction of personal information, or raise a privacy concern, email info@verticsecurity.com.au.

Updates

This policy may be updated when our services, systems or legal obligations change. Last updated: 27 August 2026.
