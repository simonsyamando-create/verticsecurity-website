About Vertic Security

Independent advice. Practical outcomes.

Vertic Security is an Australian security consultancy focused on helping organisations make informed decisions and deliver effective, scalable security outcomes.

Security should support the mission, not obstruct it.

We work with clients to understand threats, operational needs, stakeholder expectations and project constraints before defining a proportionate solution.

Our service extends from early strategy and risk assessment through design, procurement support, construction assurance, commissioning and operational review.

Our approach

  • Understand the operating environment
  • Define risk and required outcomes
  • Develop coordinated, buildable solutions
  • Support decisions with clear documentation
  • Verify that delivery meets intent
